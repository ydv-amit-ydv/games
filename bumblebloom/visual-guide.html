Game 3 · visual guide

Bumblebloom

Buzz between flowers with one button. Circle a flower to make it bloom in your colour, grow vines between your flowers, and bump rival bees off theirs.

1–8 players one button each 2-min rounds ages 6+
Live demo: 4 bot bees are playing. Hold Space or press and hold the garden to take over the red bee.
Controls

The whole game is one button

Every bee repeats the same four steps all round long.

1 · CIRCLEbutton up

Buzz around a flower

Your bee circles by itself, a little faster every second.

2 · TAKE OFFbutton down

Hold the button

Your bee flies off in the direction it was moving, keeping its speed.

3 · FLYbutton down

Keep holding

Fly straight over flowers you don't want.

4 · LANDbutton up

Let go

Your bee lands on the nearest flower in reach and keeps circling the same way.

Rules

Pollinate, grow vines, bump

Circle a flower to make it bloom

A white flower takes one full loop. A rival's flower takes two, which gives the owner time to fly back and bump you off. Leaving early resets your progress.

Vines join your flowers into honey patches

Your flowers that are close together grow vines between them. A patch of n flowers makes n × n honey per second. Take the middle flower of a rival's patch to cut their vine.

Flying beats circling

Fly into a bee that's circling and it goes dizzy for 1.6 seconds, then reappears on one of its own flowers. Two flying bees just bounce off each other.

Watch out for the flytrap

Its sweet smell pulls flying bees towards it. Use the pull to curve around a rival, but fly too close and it snaps you up.

Up to 8 players, one device

Everyone gets one key

The keys are spread across the keyboard so 8 people can crowd around one laptop.

  • Keyboard: one key per player, rebindable.
  • Gamepads: up to 8, any button.
  • Tablet or phone: the screen edge splits into one touch zone per player.
  • Mix them: press your button in the lobby to join. Empty seats can be filled with bot bees.
