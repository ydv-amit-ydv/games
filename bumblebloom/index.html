How honey is counted

Every second, each patch of your flowers joined by vines gives flowers × flowers honey. Flowers close together grow vines between them.

1 flower on its own+1 / s
2 joined flowers2 × 2 = +4 / s
3 joined flowers3 × 3 = +9 / s
4 joined flowers4 × 4 = +16 / s
5 joined flowers5 × 5 = +25 / s

Fortified: a patch of 4 or more grows a hedge, and its flowers take 3 loops to steal instead of 2.

Sting dash (button or E): your flight becomes a fast dash that hits the first rival it touches for 35 health. Knock them out within 4 s and their dropped honey is yours. 8 s cooldown.

So one big patch beats many small ones. Steal the middle flower of a rival's patch to split it in two. The label on each patch shows what it earns.
